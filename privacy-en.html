Privacy Policy

BestSwipe (the “App”)　Effective date: September 11, 2026

Languages: 日本語 ・ 한국어 ・ English

This policy explains how the provider of the App (“we,” “us”) handles your information when you use the App. The App is built around a single principle: your photos are organized and deleted entirely on your own device.

1. Who we are

  • Provider: Sakuraya Toshiki (桜谷俊輝)
  • Contact: rebirth.sakuraya@gmail.com

Business address and phone number are disclosed on the Japanese tokushoho (Specified Commercial Transactions Act) page as required under Japanese law.

2. Our core principle: on-device processing

All of the App’s processing — loading photos, sorting them, deciding which are candidates for deletion, and comparing similar shots — happens on your device. We do not transmit, collect, or store your photos, photo thumbnails, photo location data (EXIF), or any face-related information on our servers or anywhere off your device.

3. Information we do not collect

The App does not send the following off your device, and we do not collect it:

  • Your photos and videos, or their thumbnails
  • Location data contained in your photos (EXIF latitude and longitude, and similar)
  • Facial recognition or face-matching data (the App does not score or match faces)
  • Contacts, email addresses, or other account details (the App requires no account)

4. Information we may collect

Account: No account is required. The App does not request your name, email address, or phone number.

Usage analytics: We send PostHog a randomly generated installation ID; app launches, screen views, interactions and permission states; language and device-region settings; App Store storefront; app and technical versions; product and transaction IDs, price and currency; and aggregate processing times, cleanup counts and size ranges. We never send photos, videos, photo IDs, file names, album names, EXIF or face information. Although the analytics ID contains no name, it links usage and purchase records for the same installation. IP-based geolocation enrichment is disabled for PostHog captures.

Purchases: Apple processes payments. RevenueCat manages purchase, renewal, refund, expiration, product and transaction records. We do not receive card numbers. We associate RevenueCat purchase events with the PostHog installation ID for analytics.

Advertising measurement: Only after you allow App Tracking Transparency (ATT) do we initialize the Meta SDK and share advertising identifiers (IDFA), device and SDK identifiers, IP address, app activity and purchase data with Meta to measure and optimize ads for this App. Purchase events are sent through RevenueCat. The SDK may process diagnostic data. Before permission or when permission is denied, we do not start sending identifiable Meta events; Apple's SKAdNetwork provides aggregate attribution without identifying individual users. Denying ATT does not restrict photo cleanup or purchases.

Apple Ads measurement: We send the advertising attribution token issued by Apple AdServices to RevenueCat. RevenueCat retrieves campaign, ad group and keyword information from Apple and associates it with purchase records to analyze purchases and revenue by ad source. Standard attribution is collected without waiting for ATT permission. No photos or videos are sent for this process.

5. Why the App accesses your photo library

The App accesses your photo library only so that you can organize, delete, and sort your own photos into albums. Access is based on your permission, and you choose its scope — full access, or only the photos you select (Limited Access). If you grant access to selected photos only, the App works normally within that scope.

6. How deletion works

Photos are never deleted instantly. The deletion candidates you choose by swiping always appear on a review screen first, where you can confirm them. Actual deletion only happens after the standard iOS deletion confirmation. Deleted photos remain in the iOS “Recently Deleted” album for about 30 days, and you can restore them yourself within that window. We do not collect the contents of deleted photos.

7. Sharing and third-party services

We use Apple for payment, RevenueCat for purchase management, PostHog for usage analytics and Meta for advertising measurement with ATT permission. These providers receive the data described in Section 4 for those purposes. We do not share photos, videos, photo IDs, EXIF or face information with them. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Provider policies: Apple, RevenueCat, PostHog, Meta.

8. Data storage and retention

Cleanup history and settings are stored on your device. Analytics and purchase records are retained by the relevant services for as long as needed for service delivery, analysis, support, refunds and legal obligations, then deleted or aggregated according to retention settings and applicable law. Uninstalling the App does not delete analytics or purchase information already sent to these services.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the App, please contact us at the address above.

10. Your rights and data deletion

To request deletion, email rebirth.sakuraya@gmail.com with the subject “BestSwipe data deletion”. If you made a purchase, include information that helps locate the records, such as the purchase date, product name and Apple transaction information. Do not send passwords, card numbers, photos or videos. We may ask for more information to identify the relevant records. After verification, we will explain the outcome of deletion or restriction, subject to legal retention obligations. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription. Manage cancellation in iOS Settings under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

You can change advertising-tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Once the App reads your changed permission, it stops new identifiable advertising events. Contact us to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of previously sent data. We may ask for transaction information to locate the relevant records. We respond according to applicable law, subject to obligations to retain certain information.

11. Security

By keeping photo processing on your device, the App structurally reduces the risk of your photo information leaking off the device. That said, no method of processing on the internet or on a device can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. International transfers

Your photos and videos are not transferred abroad. Usage, purchase and advertising data described in Section 4 may be processed by Apple, RevenueCat, PostHog and Meta in the United States and other countries. Our PostHog project is hosted in the US region. Provider safeguards and agreements apply to these transfers.

13. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as laws change or as we add features to the App. For material changes, we will notify you within the App or through the App Store listing. Revisions take effect when posted on this page.

14. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact rebirth.sakuraya@gmail.com.
